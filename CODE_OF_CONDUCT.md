# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Pragma community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on evidence, user impact, and technical tradeoffs;
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes;
- prioritizing the health of the community over individual advantage.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- intimidation, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Pragma project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Do not open a public issue containing sensitive incident details. The repository does not currently
have GitHub private reporting enabled. Open a
[confidential contact request](https://github.com/pqpo/pragma/issues/new?template=security_contact.yml)
containing no incident details and identify it only as a Code of Conduct matter. A maintainer will
arrange a private channel.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned
with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation
against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, intent, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people
when deciding a response. Possible responses include a private correction, warning, temporary
restriction, removal of content, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
